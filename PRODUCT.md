## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS (user choice). No build step; must host on any static host (cPanel, Netlify, Vercel) and be easy for a freelance developer to hand over.

## Users

- Factory and compliance managers (garment and industrial plants) who must buy or replace fire protection equipment, often to close audit findings.
- Contractors and MEP engineers sourcing valves, hydrant gear, pumps and extinguishers for installation projects.

Both arrive knowing the part they need by its trade name (gate valve, breeching inlet, jockey pump) and want to confirm DezaCo supplies it, see the variants, and get a price.

## Product Purpose

DezaCo's website is a product catalog for fire safety equipment. Success is a buyer finding the right item quickly and sending a price enquiry.

## Positioning

DezaCo makes the products it sells (user-stated: "DezaCo makes these same products"). The catalog is DezaCo's own range, not a reseller list.

## Operating Context

- Buyers browse on phones as often as desktops; WhatsApp is a normal channel for B2B enquiries in the target markets.
- Enquiry model: buyers add items to a quote list and send it by WhatsApp or email. No online checkout, no public prices.

- Hydrant & hose: Pillar Hydrant, Oblique Landing Valve, Angle Hose Valve, Jacket Lay-flat Fire Hose, Hydrant Box, Fire Hose Reel, Breeching Inlet, Hose Rack, Fog Nozzle
- Fire extinguishers: ABC Dry Powder, CO2, Wet Chemical, Foam, Water Spray
- Fire pump systems: Jockey Pump, Electric Fire Pump, Diesel Fire Pump, Pump Control Panel, Fire Pump Flow Meter (flow meter unconfirmed: photo supplied but not circled)
- Gas suppression: pointed to in the brochure markup, unconfirmed

## Product Principles

1. Trade names first: every product is findable by the name a buyer already uses.
2. Enquiry is one tap away from any product.
3. Make nothing up: no certifications, stats or clients until the client confirms them.
4. Phone-first: the catalog must work on a mid-range Android over mobile data.
